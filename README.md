# Dapur Kilat

Dapur Kilat is an original, offline Indonesian warung cooking game: choose a customer ticket, follow its recipe card, tap ingredients to fill the wok, and serve the finished plate before the countdown expires. Play by clicking tickets and ingredient cards (keys 1–9 also select ingredients), click the ready plate or press Space to serve, press X to discard a mix, M to mute, and R to restart; keep three lives through the shift and build a speedy serving combo.
